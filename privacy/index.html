Browsing the site

The pages are plain files with no tracking, no cookies and no advertising. The company that hosts the site may log visits the way any web host does. The two typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. Everything else, the short films on the home page, the calculator and the booking page included, comes from this site.

The calculator

The calculator does its arithmetic in your browser. What you type isn't sent anywhere. The print and mail buttons hand the result to your own printer or mail program, and only what you then choose to send leaves your device.

If you email me

I get your email address and whatever you write. I use it to reply to you and for nothing else. I don't sell it or pass it to anyone.

If you book a call

For now you book a call by email. The button on the booking page only opens your own mail program, and nothing on this site collects what you write. Your email reaches my mailbox on Microsoft 365, and I treat it like any other email, as above.
